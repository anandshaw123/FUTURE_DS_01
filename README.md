## 🧭 Project Overview
The **E-Commerce Business Sales Dashboard** is an end-to-end **Business Intelligence project** built using **Power BI and Excel** to analyze sales performance, profitability, order quantity, and customer behavior.

#### An e-commerce business owner wants to understand:
- Are **sales and profits growing or declining**?
- Which **products and categories** are driving revenue?
- Which **regions and states** perform best?
- How do **shipping types** impact sales contribution?
- Which **customer segments** generate the highest value?

This dashboard converts raw E-Commerce sales data into **real-time KPIs**, **YoY/YTD comparisons**, and **interactive insights** across **categories, regions, shipping modes, and customer segments**, enabling **data-driven strategic decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Track **Year-to-Date (YTD)** Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Profit Margin  
- Analyze **Year-over-Year (YoY)** performance by category  
- Identify **Top & Bottom performing products**  
- Understand **regional and state-level sales distribution**  
- Evaluate **shipping type contribution** to revenue  
- Compare performance across **customer segments**

---

## ❓ Business Questions
1. What is the overall **sales and profit performance YTD**?  
2. Which **product categories** are growing or declining YoY?  
3. Who are the **top 5 and bottom 5 products** by YTD sales?  
4. Which **regions and states** generate the highest revenue?  
5. How does **shipping type** impact total sales?  
6. Which **customer segment** contributes most to profit?

## 🔄 End-to-End Workflow
1. **Data Import** – Loaded raw e-commerce data into Excel  
2. **Data Cleaning** – Removed inconsistencies and formatted fields  
3. **Data Modeling** – Created relationships in Power BI  
4. **DAX Measures** – Built YTD Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Margin metrics  
5. **Dashboard Design** – Created interactive and executive-ready visuals  
6. **Insight Generation** – Translated numbers into business insights

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs
- **YTD Sales:** $11.53M (▼ 0.83%)  
- **YTD Profit:** $1.34M (▲ 4.50%)  
- **YTD Quantity:** 107.2K (▼ 7.29%)  
- **Profit Margin:** 11.58% (▲ 5.37%)

---

## 💡 Key Insights

| Area | Insight |
|----|----|
| 📦 Category | Office Supplies leads sales; Technology shows YoY decline |
| 🏆 Products | A small set of products contributes disproportionately to total revenue |
| 🌍 Region | West & East regions dominate sales |
| 🚚 Shipping | Standard Class contributes highest sales share |
| 📉 Quantity | Despite profit growth, overall quantity sold has declined |
| 👥 Segment | Consumer segment generates highest revenue |

---

## 🧩 Business Recommendations
1. Improve performance of **declining categories** with promotions  
2. Focus inventory planning on **top-selling products**  
3. Investigate **quantity drop** despite margin improvement  
4. Optimize **shipping strategy** for cost vs profitability  
5. Expand marketing efforts in **high-performing regions**  
6. **Use segment insights** to tailor offers for Consumer vs Corporate customers
